# NPS Feedback Analysis: Streak Break & Retention

> For: Marcus. Source: 10 raw NPS comments (unprompted, open text). n=10, qualitative — directional, not statistical.

## Themes Mentioned More Than Once, Ranked by Frequency

| Rank | Theme | Mentions | Comments |
|------|-------|----------|----------|
| 1 | Streak break feels catastrophic and irreversible, leading to disengagement or churn | 5 | "hit a 20-day streak, missed one day, and it reset to zero... haven't opened the app since"; "no way to recover it"; "wish it would make coming back easier instead of making me feel like I failed"; "deleted after 3 weeks... lost its meaning"; "streak is the only thing keeping me engaged, but the second I lost it, I was done" |
| 2 | Explicit ask for a forgiveness/recovery mechanism (streak freeze, "coach not scorekeeper") | 3 | "I want Streakly to feel like a coach... not a scorekeeper that punishes me"; "Other apps let you freeze a streak. Why not this one?"; "make coming back easier instead of... start from scratch" |
| 3 | Notifications feel nagging, random, or excessive | 2 | "the daily reminder just started to feel like nagging"; "got three in one afternoon and just turned them all off" |
| 4 | Praise for content/lesson quality | 2 | "The first week was genuinely fun"; "Love the lessons" |
| 5 | Home screen doesn't acknowledge the user's actual state or progress | 2 | "looks the same whether I'm on a 2-day streak or coming back after two weeks away. Nothing acknowledges where I am"; "make me feel like I failed and have to start from scratch" |

**Single mention, not counted above but worth flagging:** one user said they forget the app exists after a few days and would return "if it pulled me back with something useful" — a re-engagement gap distinct from the streak-break complaints.

## Praise vs. Complaints

**Praise (2 of 10 comments):**
- "The first week was genuinely fun."
- "Love the lessons."

**Complaints (8 of 10 comments):** everything else — concentrated almost entirely on the streak-break experience (5 comments), notification quality (2 comments), and lack of state-awareness on the home screen (2 comments, one overlapping with streak-break).

**Read:** the product itself (lessons, first-week experience) is well-liked. The complaints are not about content quality — they're about what happens around a missed day: the reset, the messaging, and the lack of any path back.

## Top 3 Actionable Issues

1. **No recovery mechanism after a streak break.** The single most-repeated complaint (5 of 10 comments) and the most direct churn driver ("the second I lost it, I was done"; "deleted after 3 weeks"). Users explicitly compare Streakly unfavorably to competitors with a streak-freeze feature.
2. **Notification tone and frequency reinforce a "punisher" feel instead of a "coach" feel.** Users want to be pulled back in, not scolded — "coach that helps me get back on track, not a scorekeeper that punishes me." Current notifications are also reported as too frequent/random, leading users to disable them entirely.
3. **The home screen doesn't reflect where a user actually is.** No distinction between a 2-day streak and returning after two weeks away — a missed opportunity to acknowledge progress or ease someone back in, and part of why a break feels like starting over rather than picking up.
